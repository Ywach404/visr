VISR

Experience Halo: Combat Evolved in a whole new dimension

VISR is a native port of Halo: Combat Evolved for Apple Vision Pro and other Apple devices: iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac. Based on a lot of hard work from the community, optimized for Metal and Apple Silicon.

VISR aims to provide the best possible way to experience Halo while staying faithful to the original game. VISR builds on my love of the game and the many thousands of hours I have spent playing it.

A premium experience on every device

  • Cross-device sync

    Your saves and player profiles sync across all your devices through iCloud. Zero setup required.

  • It just works

    VISR is specifically optimized for each Apple device for unmatched performance out of the box.

  • Compatible with the community

    VISR aims for cross-play with the popular Halo: CE ports like OpenCE and Chupathingy.

  • Permissive license

    All VISR code is Apache-2.0, so you can use it in any port, mod, tool or server, under any license.

Play on whichever platform you choose

Apple Vision Pro

  • Full stereo VR
  • Head tracking and foveated rendering
  • PlayStation VR2 Sense gunplay
  • M2 or M5, visionOS 2 or later

iPhone

  • Touch controls built in
  • Full controller support
  • Play on a TV via cable or AirPlay
  • iPhone 8 or newer, iOS 16 or later

iPad

  • Touch controls built in
  • Full controller support
  • Play on a TV via cable or AirPlay
  • iPadOS 16 or later

Apple TV

  • Split-screen multiplayer on one TV
  • Full controller support
  • Apple TV HD or 4K, tvOS 16 or later

Mac

  • Native app
  • Keyboard and mouse
  • Windowed or full screen
  • Any Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later
